Major Milestones

Tracking big wins: development milestones

Walking Timeline

9-18 Months
  • 9-12 months: Pulls to stand, cruises along furniture
  • 12-14 months: Takes first independent steps
  • 15-18 months: Walks confidently, begins to run and climb

Talking Milestones

Babbling (4-6 months)

Strings vowels together: "ba-ba", "ma-ma", responds to your voice

First Words (10-14 months)

Meaningful words like "mama", "dada", "ball", "no" (1-3 words)

Two-Word Phrases (18-24 months)

Combines words: "more milk", "daddy go", "my toy" (50+ words)

Motor Skills Checklist

Fine Motor Skills
  • Grasps rattle (3 months)
  • Transfers objects hand-to-hand (6 months)
  • Pincer grasp (9 months)
  • Stacks 2 blocks (12 months)
Gross Motor Skills
  • Rolls over (4-6 months)
  • Sits without support (6-8 months)
  • Crawls (7-10 months)
  • Walks independently (12-18 months)

When to Consult a Doctor

  • No babbling by 12 months
  • No meaningful words by 16 months
  • No walking by 18 months
  • Loss of previously acquired skills
  • Not responding to their name by 12 months
