Diapering 101

Less mess, more success

Changing Routine Tips

  1. Gather supplies before starting: clean diaper, wipes, barrier cream, change of clothes
  2. Place baby on a flat, safe surface with one hand on them at all times
  3. Wipe front to back for girls to prevent infections
  4. Let skin air dry for a minute before putting on new diaper
  5. Change every 2-3 hours during the day, before/after naps

Diaper Rash Prevention

Do apply a thin layer of zinc oxide barrier cream at every change
Do change wet/dirty diapers promptly
Do give diaper-free time daily for 10-15 minutes
Don't use scented wipes or soaps on the diaper area
Don't fasten diapers too tightly around the waist

Diaper Bag Checklist

  • 3-4 diapers (1 more than you think you need)
  • Travel pack of wipes
  • Small tube of barrier cream
  • Change of clothes (onesie + outfit)
  • Disposable changing pad
  • Plastic bags for soiled clothes
