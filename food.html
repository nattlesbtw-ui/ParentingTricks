Food & Nutrition

Feeding your little one: tips for every age

Introducing Solids Timeline

  • 0-6 months: Breast milk or formula only
  • 6 months: Start iron-rich purees (rice cereal, pureed meat, sweet potato)
  • 7-9 months: Introduce soft lumps, well-cooked veggies, fruits
  • 10-12 months: Finger foods, small pieces of soft table food

Finger Foods Guide

  1. 6-9 Months
    • Soft avocado slices
    • Steamed carrot sticks
    • Banana pieces
  2. 9-12 Months
    • Cheerios
    • Small pasta shapes
    • Soft cheese cubes
  3. 12+ Months
    • Small sandwich triangles
    • Berries
    • Soft cooked veggie pieces

Meal Prep Tips

Steam and puree in bulk, freeze in ice cube trays for 1oz portions. Thaw overnight in fridge.

Store purees in fridge for 2-3 days, freezer for up to 3 months. Label with date.

Pre-cut soft fruits (banana, avocado) and store in airtight containers for grab-and-go snacks.
