Clothing & Dressing

Dressing made easy: comfort and practicality

Dressing Tips by Age

Use zip-up sleepers instead of snaps for easier nighttime changes. Avoid clothes with strings or hoods for safe sleep.

Choose pants with built-in feet or knee patches. Avoid clothes with buttons on the back once they start rolling.

Let them pick between 2 options to encourage independence. Choose elastic waistbands and slip-on shoes.

Seasonal Guide

Summer

Lightweight cotton onesies, sun hats with brims, UV-protective swimwear, breathable sandals

Winter

Fleece sleep sacks, mittens with clips, booties that stay on, layered outfits (onesie + footed pajamas)

Rainy Season

Waterproof jackets with hoods, quick-dry fabrics, extra pairs of socks, splash-proof booties
